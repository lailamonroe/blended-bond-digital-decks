How to use

You don't need to use every prompt or finish in one sitting. Let the conversation move at the pace of your family.

These prompts can be used:

  • Around the dinner table
  • Before bed
  • During one-on-one time
  • In the car or on a walk

Pause when needed. Skip anything that doesn't feel right.

There's no right way to have these conversations, only what feels right for your family.
